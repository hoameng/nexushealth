Privacy Policy for Nexus Seizure Tracker

Effective Date: May 24, 2026
Nexus Neurology ("we," "us," or "our") developed the Nexus Seizure Tracker app as a tool to help patients manage epilepsy through data logging and analytics. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.
1. No Data Collection
We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information, health data, or device data to our servers or any third parties.
2. Local Storage Only
All data you enter into the app—including seizure logs, medication regimens, adherence history, menstrual cycle dates, and video recordings—is stored locally on your device only.
We do not have access to this data.
If you delete the app or clear the app's data in your phone settings, all your logged information will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us.
3. Permissions Used
The app requests specific permissions to provide its core features. These are used only on your device:
Camera & Microphone: To allow you to record videos of seizure events.
Notifications & Alarms: To provide medication reminders.
Sensors/Accelerometer: To detect physical movement for the Beta Seizure Detector feature.
4. Third-Party Services
The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
5. Data Security
Because your data never leaves your device, the security of your information depends on the security of your physical smartphone. We recommend using device-level security (passcodes, biometrics) to protect your data.
6. Children's Privacy
Since we do not collect any data, our app does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children.
7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the version number within the app.
